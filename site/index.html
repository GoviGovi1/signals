How signals are made
  • Coins: the ~40 most traded coins that have both an OKX USDT spot pair and perpetual (at least 60 days old), refreshed daily. Data: public OKX candles.
  • Chart signal: 4-hour trend filter (EMA50 vs EMA200), then a 1-hour candle that closes beyond the high (long) or low (short) of the previous 20 hours, on the right side of the 1-hour EMA50, with RSI(14) at 55–75 for longs or 25–45 for shorts. Spot is long only.
  • Stop loss: 2 × ATR(14) from entry (“1R”). Take profits: 1R, 2R, 3R, a third at each; after TP1 the stop moves to entry. Anything left closes after 48 hours.
  • Token unlocks (DefiLlama): a cliff unlock of 2%+ of circulating supply, or $10M+ (and 0.5%+), within 7 days counts as big. It lowers the catalyst score of longs and raises shorts; after a big unlock passes and price holds, longs score higher.
  • Derivatives (OKX): open interest up 5%+ in 24h with the breakout = confirmation; OI down 10%+ after the move = exhaustion; funding of 0.05%+ per 8h paid by your side = crowded.
  • Fundamentals (DefiLlama): TVL, fees or DEX volume up 20%+ week over week while price is up less than 5% = momentum the price hasn't caught.
  • Sectors (CoinGecko): median 7-day change of each sector's top 50 coins; coins in leading sectors score higher for longs.
  • On-chain spot: GeckoTerminal trending pools on Solana, Base, BSC, Ethereum and Arbitrum with liquidity over $250k, 7+ days old, $250k+ daily volume and a GoPlus safety check. A signal fires on a 20-hour breakout of the DEX chart. These can't be backtested with free data, so treat them as the riskiest.
  • Filters vs. info: a catalyst only blocks signals if it improved results in both halves of the 90-day backtest. Everything else is shown as a reason on the card and doesn't block anything. See the backtest section for which is which.
  • Perps leverage capped at 3x. One open signal per coin per market, at most 15 open at once. Results don't include fees or funding.
