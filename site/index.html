How signals are made
  • Coins: up to 350 of the most traded crypto coins on OKX (USDT spot pair and/or perpetual, combined 24h volume over $0.25M, chart market 30+ days old; no stablecoins, wrapped or leveraged tokens), refreshed daily. The list is shorter when fewer coins clear that bar. A coin already on the list stays while it remains inside the top 450. Data: public OKX candles.
  • Hot movers: a separate scan of every OKX USDT book, not only the chart list. A long can fire when a coin is up 20%+ in 24 hours, that book did at least $0.5M, it is at least 2 days old, and volume is at least 1.8× its recent average. At most 2 per run and 3 per day. The stop is 2× the 1-hour ATR (between 4% and 12%) with targets at 1R, 2R and 3R. These are not the backtested chart setups, and they skip the 45-day coin gate on purpose so a new listing can show up. Spot vs perps uses the same rules as other signals.
  • Spot or perps: every signal says where to trade it. Shorts are perps only. Longs default to spot; they move to perps only when the coin has a liquid perp, the trend is strong, funding is cheap (≤0.01% per 8h) and the coin isn't too volatile. Leverage is set so liquidation sits far beyond the stop, max 3x; thin or wild coins get 1x. Spot-only coins get spot longs only.
  • Strategy v3 extra rules: rules such as the BTC trend, strength vs BTC and breakout volume are only switched on if they improved results after fees in both halves of the 200-coin backtest (see the table below). At most 8 new signals a day; when several fire at once the ones strongest vs BTC (or weakest, for shorts) go first.
  • Coin gate: each day, chart signals are allowed only on coins whose signals made money after fees over the last 45 days.
  • Chart reading (v4): support/resistance from swing highs/lows (1h and 4h) and high-volume price zones; market structure (higher highs/lows, break of structure); 1d/4h/1h trend; range/base detection. Setups: range breakout, retest of a broken level that holds, break of structure, and the 20-hour breakout. Stops go beyond the nearest level (1–4 ATR), targets sit just before the next levels. A signal is skipped if the next level is closer than 2R or the coin moves more than 2.5% an hour. The chart image marks the levels and the range.
  • v2 rule (still one of the setups): 4-hour trend filter (EMA50 vs EMA200), then a 1-hour candle that closes beyond the high (long) or low (short) of the previous 20 hours, on the right side of the 1-hour EMA50, with RSI(14) at 55–75 for longs or 25–45 for shorts. Spot is long only.
  • Stop loss: just beyond the nearest support (long) or resistance (short), 1–4 ATR away; 2 × ATR(14) when there is no nearby level. That distance is “1R”. Take profits: just before the next levels (at least 1R, at most 6R), or 1R/2R/3R when there are no levels. Exit plan (v4.1): 25% at TP1, 25% at TP2, 50% at TP3, and after TP1 the stop moves to entry (signals before Oct 23, 2026 used a third at each). In the 90-day backtest this beat thirds after fees in both halves. Anything left closes after 48 hours.
  • Token unlocks (DefiLlama): a cliff unlock of 2%+ of circulating supply, or $10M+ (and 0.5%+), within 7 days counts as big. It lowers the catalyst score of longs and raises shorts; after a big unlock passes and price holds, longs score higher.
  • Derivatives (OKX): open interest up 5%+ in 24h with the breakout = confirmation; OI down 10%+ after the move = exhaustion; funding of 0.05%+ per 8h paid by your side = crowded.
  • Fundamentals (DefiLlama): TVL, fees or DEX volume up 20%+ week over week while price is up less than 5% = momentum the price hasn't caught.
  • Sectors (CoinGecko): median 7-day change of each sector's top 50 coins; coins in leading sectors score higher for longs.
  • News (live filter, v4.1): free headlines from CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, The Block, Decrypt, CryptoSlate and OKX listing/delisting announcements, matched to coins by ticker or name. Fresh (24h) bearish news (hack, exploit, delisting, lawsuit, SEC action…) blocks new longs on that coin, and fresh bullish news (listing, partnership, ETF approval, mainnet, upgrade…) blocks new shorts (BTC/ETH headlines are market-wide, so they're shown but don't block). The headline and link appear in the signal's “Why”, and open signals get a short follow-up when flagged news hits the coin (at most 2 per signal and 6 a day). News can't be backtested with free data.
  • On-chain spot: GeckoTerminal trending pools on Solana, Base, BSC, Ethereum and Arbitrum with liquidity over $250k, 7+ days old, $250k+ daily volume and a GoPlus safety check. A signal fires on a 20-hour breakout of the DEX chart. These can't be backtested with free data, so treat them as the riskiest.
  • Filters vs. info: a catalyst only blocks signals if it improved results in both halves of the 90-day backtest. Everything else is shown as a reason on the card and doesn't block anything. See the backtest section for which is which.
  • Perps leverage capped at 3x. One open signal per coin per market, at most 15 open chart signals at once (hot movers have their own cap of 3). Live results don't include fees or funding; the v3 backtest does include fees. Each new signal comes with a chart image (entry, stop and targets).
