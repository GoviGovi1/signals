How signals are made
  • Data: public OKX candles (spot and USDT perpetuals), checked on the 1-hour chart.
  • Trend filter: on the 4-hour chart, EMA50 above EMA200 means only longs; below means only shorts (perps). Spot is long only.
  • Entry: a 1-hour candle closes beyond the high (long) or low (short) of the previous 20 hours, on the right side of the 1-hour EMA50, with RSI(14) at 55–75 for longs or 25–45 for shorts.
  • Stop loss: 2 × ATR(14) from entry. That distance is “1R”.
  • Take profits: TP1 at 1R, TP2 at 2R, TP3 at 3R. A third is taken at each, and after TP1 the stop moves to entry.
  • Time limit: whatever is left closes after 48 hours (“expired”).
  • Perps leverage is capped at 3x. Funding rate is shown on each perps signal.
  • One open signal per coin per market at a time. Results don't include fees or funding.
